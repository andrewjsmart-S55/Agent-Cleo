## The Challenge Every Entrepreneur Faces

I run multiple businesses - DecideWright, Studio55London, SparkwireMedia, ThinTanks, and more. Each has its own products, customers, and demands. Like most entrepreneurs, I'm great at starting things. The challenge? Finishing them. And doing it all without cloning myself.

For years, I've been seeking the answer to a fundamental question: *How do I scale myself without sacrificing quality or burning out?*

Today, I'm starting a new experiment. I'm building something I'm calling **Agent-Cleo** - an AI-powered agent orchestration system. And I'm documenting the entire journey publicly.

## What Am I Building?

Agent-Cleo isn't just another AI assistant. It's a complete **multi-agent orchestration system** - think of it as building an entire company of AI specialists, each with defined roles, responsibilities, and expertise.

Here's the architecture I'm implementing:

**27 Specialized AI Agents across 4 tiers:**

1. **Master Orchestration (1 agent)** - Agent-Cleo directs everything
2. **Personal & Team Agents (8 agents)** - Strategic direction for each business unit
3. **Worker Agents (9 agents)** - Tactical execution (CMO, Legal, EA, Product, Finance, Sales, etc.)
4. **Expert Agents (10 agents)** - Subject matter expertise (RegTech, Data Science, Cybersecurity, ESG, etc.)

Each agent has a specific role, reports to the right manager, and collaborates with other agents - just like a real organization.

## The Integration That Changes Everything

What makes this different from just "using ChatGPT"? **Real integration with my actual tools.**

Agent-Cleo connects to:
- **Todoist** - Reading and creating tasks, managing my goals
- **Telegram** - Mobile access anywhere
- **Flask Dashboard** - Monitoring and job scheduling
- **Context Management** - Each agent maintains its own knowledge base

The agents aren't just generating text. They're:
- Reading my goals from Todoist
- Creating tasks aligned with those goals
- Tracking completion rates
- Holding me accountable
- Executing work across my portfolio
