## Notes on Selection Criteria

These websites and blogs were selected based on:
- **Traffic & Reach:** Domain authority, social media following, monthly visitors
- **Content Quality:** Regular updates, expert contributors, comprehensive coverage
- **Industry Authority:** Recognition, awards, professional standing
- **UK Focus:** Either UK-based or strong UK audience/content
- **Accessibility:** Publicly available content (noted where subscription required)
