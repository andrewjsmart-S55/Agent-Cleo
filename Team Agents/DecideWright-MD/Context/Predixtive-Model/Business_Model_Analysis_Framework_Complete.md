## Integration with Value Orchestration Canvas

This comprehensive framework maps business model components to the Value Orchestration Canvas dimensions:

### **Economics → Business Model Dimension**
Each business model type with its specific CSFs directly populates the Business Model dimension, providing concrete metrics for:
- **Value Creation** performance and risks
- **Value Delivery** performance and risks  
- **Value Capture** performance and risks

### **Enablers Alignment**
Business Model CSFs reveal required capabilities:
- **Culture:** Service orientation (Fee-for-Service), innovation tolerance (Freemium), quality focus (Manufacturing)
- **People:** Technical skills (SaaS), creative talent (Media), relationship management (B2B Sales)
- **Technology:** Platform infrastructure (Marketplace), content management (Media), production systems (Manufacturing)
- **Third Parties:** Franchisees (Franchise), suppliers (Wholesale), content creators (Platform)

### **Execution Impact**
Business Model drives execution priorities:
- **Processes:** Subscription renewal flows, manufacturing quality control, marketplace matching
- **Change:** Platform feature releases, franchise system updates, product portfolio evolution
- **Innovation:** New product development (Transaction), IP creation (Licensing), content creation (Media)
- **Products & Services:** Portfolio aligned with business model economics

### **Value Outcomes**
Business Model determines success metrics:
- **Annual Results:** Model-specific KPIs (GMV, AUV, LTV, ARPU, etc.)
- **Strategic Results:** Long-term model sustainability and competitive position
- **Reputation:** Brand perception shaped by model type and execution quality

### **Risk Appetite Orchestration**
Each business model requires distinct risk appetite across dimensions:
- **Freemium:** High risk tolerance for free user costs, innovation velocity
- **Manufacturing:** Lower risk tolerance for quality failures, higher for scale investment
- **Marketplace:** High risk tolerance for early liquidity challenges, trust system investments
- **Fee-for-Service:** Balanced risk appetite for utilization vs. quality trade-offs
