## Risk Dashboard Outputs

Upon completion, you will receive quantified risk exposure metrics across the following areas:

1. **Opex at Risk** – Operating expenses threatened by risk events (annualized currency)
2. **Capex at Risk** – Capital investments at risk due to overruns, cancellations, or delays
3. **Stratex at Risk** – Strategic initiative spend at risk (transformation, R&D, change)
4. **Revenue at Risk** – Annual revenue exposure to risk scenarios (by domain, region, product)
5. **Productivity Time at Risk** – FTE days potentially lost due to outages, process failures, or staffing gaps
6. **Service/Product Availability at Risk** – Uptime hours or product units threatened by disruptions
7. **Reputation at Risk** – Composite index / scenario-based % impact on customer base and value
8. **Enterprise Value at Risk** – Estimated % / currency impact on firm value under severe scenarios

### Dashboard Capabilities

Dashboards support slicing by:
- **Domain** (Economics, Enablers, Execution, Value)
- **Dimension** (all 16 VOC dimensions)
- **Region** (6 global regions)
- **Business Model** (10 model types)
- **Time** (trends and historical comparison)

Results include scenario ranges and Monte Carlo distribution views for probabilistic risk assessment.
