## Risk Dashboard Outputs

Upon completion, you will receive region-specific quantified risk exposure metrics across the following areas:

1. **Opex at Risk** – Operating expenses threatened by risk events (annualized currency, by region)
2. **Capex at Risk** – Capital investments at risk (by region)
3. **Stratex at Risk** – Strategic initiative spend at risk (by region)
4. **Revenue at Risk** – Annual revenue exposure to risk scenarios (by region and domain)
5. **Productivity Time at Risk** – FTE days potentially lost (by region)
6. **Service/Product Availability at Risk** – Uptime hours or product units threatened (by region)
7. **Reputation at Risk** – Composite index / scenario-based % impact (by region)
8. **Enterprise Value at Risk** – Estimated % / currency impact on firm value (by region)

### Dashboard Capabilities

Dashboards support slicing by:
- **Region** (each of 6 global regions independently)
- **Domain** (Economics, Enablers, Execution, Value)
- **Dimension** (all 16 VOC dimensions)
- **Business Model** (10 model types)
- **Time** (trends and historical comparison)

Results include scenario ranges and Monte Carlo distribution views for probabilistic risk assessment with full regional breakdown.

## Data Quality Guidance

### High Priority Data Points (By Region)
The following metrics provide the highest value for regional risk quantification:
- **Financial**: Revenue at Risk %, Cash Reserve Cover by region
- **Business Model**: Revenue Concentration %, Major Revenue Streams by region
- **People**: Employee Turnover Rate %, Succession Gaps by region
- **Technology**: Annual IT Downtime, Cyber Incidents by region
- **Strategic**: Off-Track Initiatives, Strategic KPIs Achieved % by region
- **Reputation**: Customer Churn Rate %, Reputation Crisis Impact % by region

### Regional Data Collection Tips
- **Start with largest revenue regions** to maximize value
- **Use regional teams** to gather local data where possible
- **Estimate if exact data unavailable** - precision improves with iteration
- **Focus on variance across regions** - differences reveal key risks

### Estimation Guidance (When Exact Data Unavailable)
- **Percentages**: Estimate to nearest 5% per region
- **Counts**: Use best judgment based on regional team input
- **Currency**: Round to nearest $1,000 for most items
- **Time periods**: Use most recent 12 months unless specified
