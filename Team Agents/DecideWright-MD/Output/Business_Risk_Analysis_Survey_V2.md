# Business Risk Analysis Survey - Version 2
## Value Orchestration Canvas Risk Assessment (Data-Driven)

---

## Survey Overview

This comprehensive risk analysis survey evaluates your organization across the 16 dimensions of the Value Orchestration Canvas framework using actual data and metrics wherever possible. This data-driven approach enables more accurate multivariate analysis and Monte Carlo simulation.

**Key Principles:**
- Provide actual numbers, percentages, and metrics when available
- If actual data is not available, you can provide estimates or use the 1-5 scale fallback
- More precise data = more accurate risk assessment

**Risk Exposure Metrics Calculated:**
- **Opex at Risk** - Operating expenses threatened
- **Capex at Risk** - Capital expenditure at risk
- **Stratex at Risk** - Strategic investments at risk
- **Revenue at Risk** - Revenue threatened by risks
- **Productivity Time at Risk** - FTE days lost
- **Service Availability at Risk** - Uptime hours threatened
- **Product at Risk** - Product delivery/quality risks
- **Reputation at Risk** - Brand and market position impact
- **Enterprise Value at Risk** - Total shareholder value impact

**Estimated Completion Time:** 30-45 minutes
