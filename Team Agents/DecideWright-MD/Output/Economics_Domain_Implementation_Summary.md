## 1. Business Model Sheet (31 Rows)

### Structure Overview

**Level 1: Business Model (Dimension)**
- How the organization creates, delivers, and captures value

**Level 2: Six Elements**
1. **Value Creation** - How offerings are designed and developed
2. **Value Delivery** - How offerings reach customers at scale
3. **Value Capture** - How value converts to revenue and profit
4. **Revenue Streams** - Different sources of revenue generation
5. **Cost Structure** - Major cost drivers and economics
6. **Business Model Resilience** - Durability and adaptability

### Level 3 Sub-Elements Highlights

#### Value Creation (4 sub-elements)
- Customer Problem Clarity - Problem-solution fit validation
- Solution Differentiation - Competitive uniqueness assessment
- Innovation Pipeline - New product revenue contribution
- Value Quantification - Customer ROI measurement

#### Value Delivery (4 sub-elements)
- Channel Effectiveness - CAC by distribution channel
- Operational Scalability - Marginal cost per customer
- Customer Experience Quality - Net Promoter Score (NPS)
- Service Level Consistency - SLA achievement rates

#### Value Capture (4 sub-elements)
- Pricing Power - Price realization vs discounting
- Revenue Model Sustainability - Annual recurring revenue %
- Customer Lifetime Value - LTV calculation and target ratios
- Monetization Alignment - Value-to-price perception

#### Revenue Streams (4 sub-elements)
- Primary Revenue Stream - Core offering growth rate
- Adjacent Revenue Streams - Portfolio diversification (HHI)
- Emerging Revenue Streams - Innovation investment %
- Revenue Stream Synergy - Cross-sell penetration rates

#### Cost Structure (4 sub-elements)
- Unit Economics - Contribution margin per customer
- Fixed vs Variable Balance - Operating leverage ratio
- Cost of Customer Acquisition - CAC and payback period
- Cost Scalability - Cost growth vs revenue growth

#### Business Model Resilience (4 sub-elements)
- Competitive Barriers - Moat strength assessment (5 factors)
- Market Position Strength - Market share and trend
- Business Model Adaptability - Innovation experiments count
- Customer Switching Costs - % of ACV to switch

### Key Metrics
- **15 quantitative metrics** (CAC, LTV, NPS, ARR%, Contribution Margin, etc.)
- **9 qualitative assessments** (differentiation scores, positioning, maturity)
- All linked to business model archetypes from the Business Model library

### Integration Points
- Links to Base Information: Business Model Type selection
- Feeds performance analysis: Revenue quality, scalability
- Feeds risk analysis: Business model disruption, value leakage

## 2. External Environment Sheet (31 Rows)

### Structure Overview

**Level 1: External Environment (Dimension)**
- External forces and conditions affecting the organization

**Level 2: Six Elements**
1. **Market Dynamics** - Market size, growth, and evolution
2. **Competitive Intensity** - Competitive pressure and dynamics
3. **Regulatory Environment** - Legal and compliance requirements
4. **Economic Conditions** - Macroeconomic factors
5. **Technology Trends** - Technology changes and disruption
6. **Socio-Political Factors** - Social and political conditions

### Level 3 Sub-Elements Highlights

#### Market Dynamics (4 sub-elements)
- Total Addressable Market (TAM) - Market opportunity size
- Market Growth Rate - CAGR of TAM (target >10% for high-growth)
- Market Maturity Stage - Emerging/Growth/Mature/Decline
- Market Fragmentation - CR4 concentration ratio

#### Competitive Intensity (4 sub-elements)
- Number of Competitors - Direct competitor count
- Competitive Differentiation - Market differentiation score (1-10)
- Pricing Pressure - Industry price trend (inflation-adjusted)
- Threat of New Entrants - Entry barrier assessment (5 factors)

#### Regulatory Environment (4 sub-elements)
- Regulatory Intensity - Light/Moderate/Heavy/Extreme classification
- Regulatory Trend - Liberalizing/Stable/Tightening assessment
- Compliance Risk Level - Violation history and exposure
- Regulatory Advantage - License/approval competitive positioning

#### Economic Conditions (4 sub-elements)
- GDP Growth Trend - Weighted average across markets
- Inflation Environment - CPI/PPI vs pricing power
- Interest Rate Environment - Cost of debt and debt service coverage
- Economic Cycle Position - Expansion/Peak/Contraction/Trough

#### Technology Trends (4 sub-elements)
- Disruptive Technology Threats - Top 3 threats + maturity
- Technology Adoption Rate - Laggard/Follower/Early adopter position
- Digital Transformation Pressure - Digital maturity score (4 dimensions)
- Technology Investment Trends - Tech investment % vs industry

#### Socio-Political Factors (4 sub-elements)
- Political Stability - Country risk scores (weighted)
- Social Trends Alignment - Brand reputation score
- Demographic Trends - Target demographic growth rates
- ESG Expectations - ESG performance score (0-100)

### Key Metrics
- **18 quantitative metrics** (TAM, CAGR, CR4, GDP growth, inflation, tech investment %)
- **6 risk assessments** (political risk, regulatory exposure, economic cycle)
- Integration with Base Information for country/region context

### Integration Points
- Links to Base Information: Industry, regions, regulatory intensity
- Feeds risk analysis: Market decline, competitive pressure, regulatory changes
- Feeds performance analysis: Market tailwinds/headwinds, competitive position

## 3. Governance Sheet (31 Rows)

### Structure Overview

**Level 1: Governance (Dimension)**
- Structures, processes, and accountability guiding the organization

**Level 2: Six Elements**
1. **Board Effectiveness** - Quality of board oversight
2. **Risk Oversight** - Enterprise risk management governance
3. **Compliance Management** - Systems ensuring legal adherence
4. **Ethical Culture** - Values promoting ethical conduct
5. **Stakeholder Governance** - Multi-stakeholder accountability
6. **Internal Controls** - Processes ensuring reliable operations

### Level 3 Sub-Elements Highlights

#### Board Effectiveness (4 sub-elements)
- Board Composition - Independence % + Expertise coverage
- Board Engagement - Meeting attendance + Preparation quality
- Board Oversight Quality - Challenge and debate effectiveness (1-10)
- Board Committee Function - Audit/Risk/Compensation effectiveness

#### Risk Oversight (4 sub-elements)
- Risk Governance Framework - ERM maturity level (1-5)
- Risk Appetite Definition - Documented, quantified, communicated
- Board Risk Reporting - Frequency + Quality score (1-10)
- Three Lines of Defense - 1st/2nd/3rd line implementation

#### Compliance Management (4 sub-elements)
- Compliance Program Maturity - Ad-hoc to Optimized (1-5)
- Compliance Training & Awareness - Completion % + Assessment scores
- Compliance Violations & Remediation - Count + Days to remediate
- Whistleblower & Reporting - Reports received + Trust score

#### Ethical Culture (4 sub-elements)
- Code of Conduct Quality - Comprehensive/Clear/Current assessment
- Leadership Tone & Behavior - Leadership ethics score (1-10)
- Conflict of Interest Management - Disclosure rate + COI count
- Ethical Decision-Making Framework - Escalation utilization + Awareness

#### Stakeholder Governance (4 sub-elements)
- Stakeholder Identification & Engagement - Count + Frequency + Satisfaction
- Shareholder Rights & Protections - Voting/Info/Treatment/Recourse
- Employee Voice & Representation - Engagement score + Trust score
- Community & Social Impact - Community satisfaction + Impact metrics

#### Internal Controls (4 sub-elements)
- Financial Controls Effectiveness - Material weaknesses + Significant deficiencies
- Operational Controls Maturity - % processes controlled + Pass rate
- IT General Controls (ITGC) - Access/Change/Backup/Operations score
- Fraud Prevention & Detection - Control score + Incidents + Losses

### Key Metrics
- **16 scored assessments** (maturity levels, effectiveness scores, quality ratings)
- **8 quantitative metrics** (attendance %, violation counts, deficiency counts)
- Strong link to compliance, audit, and risk management systems

### Integration Points
- Links to Base Information: Risk appetite, risk management maturity
- Feeds risk analysis: Governance failures, control breakdowns, compliance violations
- Feeds performance analysis: Board effectiveness, ethical culture strength
