## Training Philosophy & Approach

### **Core Principles**

**1. Sustainable Progression**
- No crash diets or extreme training programs
- Build habits that last beyond goal achievement
- Progressive overload with adequate recovery
- Listen to body signals (fatigue, soreness, pain)

**2. Time-Efficient Training**
- Maximize results per minute invested
- Minimal effective dose approach
- Strategic workout timing around business schedule
- Combined training where appropriate (e.g., karate counts as cross-training)

**3. Entrepreneur-Friendly Programming**
- Flexible scheduling for business demands
- Home workout options when travel required
- Quick decision framework for workout vs business priorities
- Recovery optimization for high-stress lifestyle

**4. Data-Driven Adjustments**
- Track key metrics consistently
- Adjust based on progress data, not feelings alone
- Regular body composition assessments
- Performance benchmarks (run times, karate skills)

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### **Marathon Training Metrics**
- Weekly mileage completed vs planned
- Long run distance progression
- Run pace improvements
- Resting heart rate (cardiovascular fitness)
- Training consistency (% of planned workouts completed)

### **Body Composition Metrics**
- Waist circumference (weekly measurement)
- Body weight (weekly weigh-in, same day/time)
- Body fat percentage (monthly assessment)
- Progress photos (monthly)
- How clothes fit (qualitative but important)

### **Karate Progression Metrics**
- Training sessions attended per week
- Belt advancement milestones
- Technique assessments from instructor
- Kata mastery (forms learned and perfected)
- Sparring skill development

### **Recovery & Health Metrics**
- Sleep quality and duration (hours per night)
- Energy levels (daily rating 1-10)
- Injury status (pain, soreness, issues)
- Stress levels (impact on training)
- Workout completion rate

## Accountability & Check-In Structure

### **Daily Brief Check** (2-3 minutes)
- Did planned workout happen? (Yes/No)
- If no, what was the obstacle?
- Energy level today (1-10 scale)
- Any pain or injury concerns?
- Tomorrow's workout confirmed in calendar?

### **Weekly Review** (10-15 minutes)
- Total workouts completed vs planned
- Total weekly mileage/training time
- Nutrition adherence (self-rated 1-10)
- Body metrics update (weight, waist measurement)
- Next week's training plan confirmation
- Adjustments needed based on this week's data

### **Monthly Assessment** (30-45 minutes)
- Comprehensive progress review (all KPIs)
- Body composition assessment
- Performance benchmarks (timed run, fitness test)
- Training plan adjustments for next month
- Goal progress check (on track? need changes?)
- Celebrate wins and analyze obstacles
