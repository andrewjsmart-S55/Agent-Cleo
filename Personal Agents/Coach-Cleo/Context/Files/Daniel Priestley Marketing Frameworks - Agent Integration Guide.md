## Framework 2: Oversubscribed (Create More Demand Than Supply)

### The Oversubscribed Formula

**Problem**: Most businesses chase customers and compete on price
**Solution**: Create campaign-driven demand that exceeds your capacity

### 7 Steps to Being Oversubscribed

#### Step 1: Choose Your Market and Set Capacity Limits

**Principle**: Don't try to serve everyone; pick a specific market and set clear capacity limits

**For DecideWright**:
- **Target Market**: Mid-market companies ($50M-$500M revenue) with complex risk portfolios
- **Capacity**: 8 QRA/VOC/TPRM projects per quarter maximum
- **Positioning**: "We only take 8 clients per quarter to ensure quality"

**For Studio55**:
- **Target Market**: Tech startups and enterprises wanting rapid AI development
- **Capacity**: 4 AI development projects simultaneously
- **Positioning**: "4-week AI sprints, 4 projects max at a time"

**For Greentabula**:
- **Target Market**: EU mid-market companies facing CSRD compliance deadlines
- **Capacity**: 6 CSRD implementations per quarter
- **Positioning**: "CSRD compliance before the deadline, limited availability"

#### Step 2: Build Up Market Data and Insights

**Principle**: Know your market better than they know themselves

**Market Data to Collect**:
- How many companies in target market?
- What % need your solution right now?
- What % will need it in next 12 months?
- What's the total addressable market (TAM)?
- What are market trends and triggers?

**For DecideWright**:
- **TAM**: Estimate mid-market companies globally with risk management needs
- **Market Trigger**: Increasing regulatory scrutiny on risk management
- **Insight**: Most companies still use RAG status (opportunity to quantify)

**Action for EAs**:
- Each business EA: Compile market data document
- Include in Context folder for reference
- Update quarterly with new insights

#### Step 3: Create a Campaign-Driven Enterprise (CDE)

**Principle**: Don't sell continuously; create events and campaigns that generate bursts of interest

**Traditional (BAD)**: "We offer QRA services, contact us anytime"
**Campaign-Driven (GOOD)**: "QRA Cohort 1 starts Jan 15th. Only 8 spots. Apply by Jan 1st."

**DecideWright Campaign Ideas**:
1. **QRA Quarterly Cohorts**: "Q1 2026 QRA Cohort - 8 Companies Maximum"
2. **VOC Sprint Week**: "5-Day Value Orchestration Canvas Intensive - March 2026"
3. **TPRM Assessment Month**: "April 2026: Free TPRM assessments, 20 slots only"

**Studio55 Campaign Ideas**:
1. **AI Sprint Weeks**: "4-Week AI Development Sprint - Starting Every Month"
2. **AI Strategy Workshops**: "Quarterly AI Strategy Intensive - 12 Companies Max"

**Greentabula Campaign Ideas**:
1. **CSRD Compliance Countdown**: "Beat the CSRD Deadline - 6 Spots Remaining"
2. **ESG Readiness Bootcamp**: "2-Day CSRD Bootcamp - April 2026"

**Key Benefits**:
- Creates urgency (limited spots, specific dates)
- Makes selling easier (batch conversations)
- Builds momentum (group energy)
- Enables scarcity (real capacity limits)

**Action for EAs**:
- DecideWright-EA: Plan Q1 2026 campaign
- S55-EA: Plan Q1 2026 AI sprint campaign
- Greentabula-EA: Plan CSRD compliance campaign
- SalesMachine-Agent (when created): Execute campaign sales process

#### Step 4: Price Your Products or Services Premium

**Principle**: Premium pricing signals premium value and creates exclusivity

**Pricing Psychology**:
- Low price = Commodity (compete with everyone)
- Mid price = Competitive (still price-sensitive)
- High price = Premium (buyers are invested in success)

**Current vs Premium Pricing**:

**DecideWright**:
- **Traditional Consulting**: $150-$200/hour = $30K for 150-200 hours
- **Premium QRA Pilot**: $15K for 24-hour transformation (better value perception)
- **Premium QRA Full**: $50K for Phase 1-4 complete transformation

**Studio55**:
- **Traditional**: $100-$150/hour development = $40K for 3-month project
- **Premium AI Sprint**: $35K for 4-week production-ready AI solution
- **Premium AI Strategy**: $50K for strategy + first implementation

**Greentabula**:
- **Traditional**: CSRD consulting at $200/hour = $40K for compliance
- **Premium CSRD Pilot**: $25K for fast-track compliance assessment
- **Premium CSRD Full**: $75K for complete CSRD compliance program

**Action for EAs**:
- Review current pricing assumptions
- Repackage services as premium fixed-price offerings
- Build case studies demonstrating ROI justifying premium pricing

#### Step 5: Build Up Your Supporting Cast

**Principle**: You can't be oversubscribed alone; build a team and ecosystem

**Andrew's Supporting Cast**:
- **EA Agents**: 7+ specialized agents handling different business areas
- **Future Hires**: Sales, delivery, operations as businesses scale
- **Partners**: Referral partners, white-label partners
- **Advisors**: Business coaches (Coach Cleo), technical advisors
- **Community**: RBPM methodology followers, LinkedIn network

**Supporting Cast Priorities**:
1. **Immediate**: Maximize EA agent effectiveness
2. **Next 3 months**: First sales hire or contractor (SalesMachine-Agent bridges this gap)
3. **Next 6 months**: First delivery/operations hire for DecideWright
4. **Next 12 months**: Build small team around each business

**Action for Coach Cleo**:
- Track when Andrew becomes the bottleneck in each business
- Push hiring/delegation decisions proactively
- Don't let Andrew be sole delivery person at scale

#### Step 6: Create Awesome Experiences

**Principle**: Oversubscribed businesses deliver experiences that customers can't stop talking about

**Experience Design Elements**:
1. **Pre-Purchase**: Make buying process feel special
2. **Onboarding**: "You made the right decision" confirmation
3. **Delivery**: Exceed expectations on core service
4. **Post-Delivery**: Maintain relationship, create referrals

**DecideWright QRA Experience Design**:
```
PRE-PURCHASE:
- Discovery call feels consultative, not salesy
- Receive custom risk assessment based on conversation
- "VIP access" to limited cohort spots

ONBOARDING:
- Welcome package with QRA Playbook, branded materials
- Kick-off call with full team introduction
- Clear 24-hour transformation roadmap

DELIVERY:
- Live risk quantification workshop (exciting, collaborative)
- Real-time dashboard showing transformation from RAG to numbers
- Executive presentation of results

POST-DELIVERY:
- Case study co-creation (with client approval)
- Quarterly check-ins on risk management progress
- First-access to QRA Phase 2-4 offerings
```

**Action for EAs**:
- Each business EA: Design complete customer experience journey
- Identify "wow moments" that create word-of-mouth
- Build templates and checklists for consistent delivery

#### Step 7: Sell Out or Raise Your Standards

**Principle**: Once oversubscribed, either sell more (scale) or be more selective (premium)

**When DecideWright Sells Out 8 Spots**:
- **Option A**: Increase capacity to 12 spots (scale)
- **Option B**: Keep 8 spots, increase price to $20K (premium)
- **Option C**: Launch Phase 2-4 for existing clients (upsell)
- **Priestley Recommendation**: Usually Option B or C (premium > scale)

**When Studio55 Sells Out 4 AI Projects**:
- **Option A**: Hire developers, increase to 6-8 projects
- **Option B**: Increase sprint price from $35K to $45K
- **Option C**: Maintain scarcity, build waiting list

**The Oversubscribed Flywheel**:
```
Scarcity → Demand → Social Proof → More Demand → Sell Out → 
Raise Prices → More Scarcity → Higher Demand → Etc.
```

**Action for Coach Cleo**:
- Don't let Andrew undervalue or over-deliver
- When demand exceeds capacity, push for price increase not capacity increase
- Scale through systems and team, not through Andrew working more hours

## Framework 3: Scorecard Marketing (Lead Generation Engine)

### The Four-Step Scorecard Process

**Problem**: Traditional marketing is interruption-based and low-conversion
**Solution**: Offer valuable assessments that qualify leads while demonstrating expertise

### Step 1: Build Your Scorecard

**What Is a Scorecard?**
- Interactive assessment or calculator
- Provides instant value to prospect
- Qualifies leads based on their answers
- Demonstrates your expertise

**DecideWright Scorecard Ideas**:
1. **Risk Management Maturity Scorecard**
   - 15 questions about current risk practices
   - Scores 0-100 on risk management maturity
   - Identifies gaps QRA/VOC/TPRM could address
   - Qualifies: Companies scoring 40-70 are ideal (aware but not advanced)

2. **Strategy Execution Health Check**
   - 20 questions about strategy execution challenges
   - Scores organizational execution capability
   - Highlights VOC methodology benefits
   - Qualifies: Companies with high strategy ambitions but low execution scores

3. **Third-Party Risk Exposure Calculator**
   - Input number of vendors, criticality, controls
   - Calculates total risk exposure in dollars
   - Shows TPRM methodology value
   - Qualifies: Companies with high exposure and weak controls

**Studio55 Scorecard Ideas**:
1. **AI Readiness Assessment**
   - Assess data quality, technical capability, use case clarity
   - Score AI implementation readiness
   - Identifies projects most likely to succeed
   - Qualifies: High-readiness companies can start immediately

2. **AI Project ROI Calculator**
   - Input current manual process costs
   - Calculate potential AI automation savings
   - Demonstrates value of AI development
   - Qualifies: High-ROI opportunities are ideal clients

**Greentabula Scorecard Ideas**:
1. **CSRD Compliance Readiness Scorecard**
   - 25 questions about current sustainability reporting
   - Scores readiness for CSRD deadline
   - Identifies gaps in compliance preparation
   - Qualifies: Companies with low scores have urgent need

**Action for EAs**:
- DecideWright-EA: Build Risk Management Maturity Scorecard (Priority 1)
- S55-EA: Build AI Readiness Assessment
- Greentabula-EA: Build CSRD Readiness Scorecard
- Technical: Simple web form + automated email with results

### Step 2: Promote Your Scorecard

**Promotion Channels**:
1. **LinkedIn**: Post about scorecard, offer free assessments
2. **Blog**: Articles that link to scorecard as resource
3. **Email Signature**: Link to scorecard in every email
4. **Speaking**: Mention scorecard in presentations
5. **Partners**: Ask partners to promote scorecard to their audience

**Promotion Copy Formula**:
```
HOOK: "Most companies think their risk management is mature..."
PROBLEM: "...but 73% fail to quantify risks in dollar terms"
SOLUTION: "Take our 5-minute Risk Management Maturity Scorecard"
CTA: "Get your free personalized assessment + recommendations"
```

**Action for EAs**:
- PersonalBrand-Agent (when created): Promote scorecards via LinkedIn
- RBPM-EA: Write blog posts linking to DecideWright scorecard
- SalesMachine-Agent (when created): Use scorecards as lead magnets

### Step 3: Follow Up With Qualified Leads

**Lead Scoring from Scorecard**:
- **Hot Lead** (90-100 score): Schedule call immediately
- **Warm Lead** (70-89 score): Nurture with case studies
- **Cool Lead** (50-69 score): Add to email list, educate over time
- **Cold Lead** (<50 score): Thank them, don't pursue

**Follow-Up Sequence for Hot Leads**:
```
Day 0: Scorecard results email with CTA to book call
Day 2: Personal video follow-up from Andrew
Day 5: Case study of similar company transformation
Day 7: Final email with limited-time offer (campaign urgency)
```

**Follow-Up Sequence for Warm Leads**:
```
Week 1: Weekly educational content on fixing their specific gaps
Week 2: Case study relevant to their industry
Week 3: Invite to webinar or workshop
Week 4: Special offer for scorecard participants
```

**Action for EAs**:
- SalesMachine-Agent (when created): Manage follow-up sequences
- Each business EA: Provide relevant case studies for follow-up
- Track conversion rates: Scorecard → Call → Client

### Step 4: Scale and Optimize

**Optimization Metrics**:
- **Scorecard Completion Rate**: What % of people who start finish?
- **Lead Quality**: What % of completions are qualified leads?
- **Conversion Rate**: What % of qualified leads become clients?
- **Cost Per Lead**: How much to acquire each scorecard completion?
- **ROI**: Revenue generated per $1 spent on scorecard promotion

**Optimization Tactics**:
- **A/B Test Questions**: Which questions best qualify leads?
- **Test Promotion Copy**: Which headlines drive most completions?
- **Refine Scoring**: Adjust score thresholds based on conversion data
- **Improve Follow-Up**: Test different sequences and content

**Action for Coach Cleo**:
- Track scorecard metrics in weekly reviews
- Push for continuous optimization
- Don't let scorecards become "set it and forget it"

## Success Metrics for Marketing Frameworks

### Key Person of Influence Scorecard (Score 1-10 for Each)
- **Pitch**: How clear and compelling is positioning?
- **Publish**: How consistent is content output?
- **Product**: How well packaged are offerings?
- **Profile**: How visible is Andrew in market?
- **Partnership**: How strong is referral network?

**Goal**: Score 8+ on all pillars within 12 months

### Oversubscribed Metrics
- Are campaigns selling out? (Target: 80%+ capacity)
- Is there a waiting list? (Target: 3-5 people waiting)
- Are prices increasing? (Target: 20% price increase when sold out)
- Is demand exceeding supply? (Target: 2x applications vs. capacity)

### Scorecard Marketing Metrics
- Scorecard completions per month (Target: 50+)
- Qualified lead % (Target: 30%+ of completions)
- Scorecard → Client conversion (Target: 10%+)
- Cost per lead (Target: <$50)
